Enhancing Feel Young (飞扬) Band

Design for Community Service Design Branding 2025
PROJECT OVERVIEW

"Co-designing with seniors in the community to turn daily friction into shared street culture."

We observed daily routines in Chinatown to spot community friction points. By directly involving local elders in co-design sessions, we crafted solutions that solve their challenges while fitting seamlessly into their environment.

Featuring "Feel Young (飞扬) Band"

Every week at 6:30 pm, a humble group of seniors gathers behind Chinatown Exit A. Together, they play instruments, sing, and dance, displaying a vibrant community spirit. While passersbys might see them at dinnertime, there is a much deeper story behind this group. We spent ten weeks with the band every Saturday, learning about their values of aging gracefully and incorporating a brand identity into their everyday routine that captures who they are.

[ OPPORTUNITY FINDING & RESEARCH ]

Conducting field research to observe seniors' routines

We chose Chinatown to begin our research as it is known for it's heritage and being a popular senior hangout spot. We observed senior routines, identifying areas to work on. Chatting with various seniors helped us understand the delicate balance they maintain between their hobbies, families, and past careers.

Persona crafting: At-a-glance of seniors' values

Senior's routines, motivations and values were jotted down in form of personas, for an easy view of what they hold important.

Seniors value graceful ageing and finding community

Insight: "Seniors maintain their dignity by staying active and finding community, which in turn allows them to step back and give their adult children space to grow."
Seniors maintain dignity through active routines.
A sense of community fills the gap of a busy family.
They have a desire for independence & self-worth.
[ Storytelling ]

The “Chinatown Band” is a community of passionate seniors

A local senior, Mr. Lok, introduced us to the retiree band performing at Chinatown Exit A. Watching them share food and care for one another revealed a strong community spirit. We joined them weekly on their performance days to get to know them better, and uncover opportunities that we could incorporate into their routines with design.

The “Feel Young”(飞扬) Spirit: Spreading joy to others through music

Each week, members take on small roles, such as setting up instruments, taping stage boundaries, and welcoming crowd members.

Insight: Their performances prioritise joy and connection over perfection, turning passersby into participants and bringing community to local old folks homes.

The Conflict: Misunderstood as noisemakers

During our fieldwork, police arrived following a noise complaint. Despite the band using smaller speakers, they were asked to pack up, revealing a gap between their positive intent and public perception.

Their goal was just to make others happy, and that’s it

Band co-leader Mr. Poh shared how hurtful it was to be dismissed as "noisemakers." This inspired us to help them communicate who they truly are through thoughtful design.

[ Co-design ]

Opportunity: Co-creation of band identity

We saw an opportunity to co-design a clear band identity so onlookers instantly recognise their purpose, building respect and preventing misunderstandings.

The individuality of each member

In our workshops, we asked each member to pick their favorite flowers and colors. These chosen blooms became personal symbols representing their individual personalities. We then paired each flower with custom motifs reflecting the member's unique role and musical talent in the group.

A bouquet of individuals

Together, these individual elements form a "bouquet", a unified brand identity celebrating both individual stories and their collective spirit.

Creating low-cost, familiar, high-impact items from their routine

We designed directly for their routine by applying their new identity to familiar items, such as the trolley, fans and maracas. We kept the look vintage and subtle so the products elevate their identity while blending naturally into the Chinatown streetscape.
