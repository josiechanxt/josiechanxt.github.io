The Bee Crisis

Data Visualisation Storytelling 2025
PROJECT OVERVIEW

“ Design and develop a set of two-panel posters that visually tell a story of either a sequence, evolution, or cycle. ”

Developed during my second year of University, this project involved translating raw complex data into structured information through 2D visualisation. I utilised graphic storytelling to create a series of visual narratives, focusing on layout, colour and hierarchy to ensure the message was legible and engaging for the target audience.

Key Skills

  • • Graphic Storytelling
  • • Conceptualising a clear and engaging narrative
  • • Idea-sketching and visual iterations

[ EXHIBITION ]

Exhibited at Singapore Design Week 2025

As part of Singapore Design Week 2025, My project was exhibited at the Singapore Science Park–Shopee Lobby, part of a themed exhibition titled "Envision". Singapore Design Week is one of the region's premier design festivals, organised by the DesignSingapore Council.

[ Storytelling ]

A sequential visual narrative

I've chosen to focus on the bee crisis, highlighting the significant issue of bee population decline that has been affecting the world for some time. The posters aim to raise awareness about the vital role of bees for us, the environment, and the threats they face from human activities.

The Problem

The bee population decline has negative consequences on the ecosystem and the crops.

The Causes

We're to blame as our activities push bees toward collapse, and the damage worsens.

The Goal

My goal is to raise awareness about bees dying as it affects us all (humans, ecosystems, crops).

[ Final Outcome ]

Final Poster Set

A SEQUENTIAL visual narrative was opted to illustrate the key factors driving bee population decline in the U.S. and Europe, and its subsequent impact on ecosystems and crops.

[ Design Process ]

Research, concepts & iterations

Translating quantitative & qualitative raw data into visual sketches

I conducted secondary research on the bee population decline and its environmental impact, then interpreted these quantitative and qualitative data into visual sketches to establish the sequence and narrative structure.

Hi-fidelity mockups with visual storytelling

Using my initial sketches, I designed high-fidelity mockups in Adobe Illustrator. I experimented with contrasting imagery (e.g thriving bees and flower vs dying bees and a decaying apple) to strengthen the visual storytelling. Ultimately, I chose a landscape layout to improve element spacing, hierarchy, and story flow.
