Underrated Trash Snackers

Project 1 • 2021

The Underrated Trash Snackers is a passion project campaign to make food waste reduction fun and educational for all ages. I envisioned a cute, sustainable art direction, using paper textures to break the perception that worms are disgusting. All assets and the case video were created by me.

🏆 This project won 4 Silvers, 2 Bronzes & 1 Finalist at the Crowbar Awards 2021.

THE Narrative 📖
With food waste making up 11% of Singapore's total waste (NEA), many want to help but lack guidance. The campaign introduces the adorable Squiggles family to lead households through vermicomposting, using worms to recycle food scraps, proving that anyone can easily turn everyday leftovers into valuable resources (e.g fertiliser for plants)

Project 2 • 2024

During my internship at The Secret Little Agency (TSLA), I worked with a copywriter intern to tackle a "Mother Brief", where we created a campaign concept to raise awareness of ongoing attacks on women’s freedom, for which I designed all visual assets.

THE Narrative 📖
Following the U.S. Supreme Court’s overturning of abortion rights, access to safe procedures has drastically diminished. Our campaign uses biting satire to spark a deeper conversation on reproductive rights, leaning into the grim irony of "D.I.Y." healthcare to expose the dangerous reality women face when denied essential medical care and left to take matters into their own hands.

National Heritage Board IG Posts

Project 3 • 2024

Designed engaging Instagram collage posts for the National Heritage Board, educating Singapore’s diverse heritage in a fun, visual way. I collaborated with a copywriter to conceptualise the posts, using images sourced from the Roots.gov.sg website for visual storytelling.
